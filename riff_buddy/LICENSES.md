## Written here rather than bundled

- **WSOLA time-stretch** (`audio/LoopRenderer.kt`, M9). The algorithm is
  Verhelst and Roelands' Waveform-Similarity Overlap-Add (1993), a published
  method, not a library. Nothing was copied from Sonic, SoundTouch or Rubber
  Band; no license applies beyond this project's own.
